## Finding 1: the 16.9% wrapper is one sample gap, not a hot loop
- Duration-weighted old profile: 67.8% of busy time sits in 22 samples longer than 50 ms. The wrapper `utils.js:3` has
  1 sample, weight 4.9 s, at 2.2 s after profile start (boot, before the drivers ran). Its stack holds no repo frame.
- Count-weighted old profile: the wrapper is 0.02% of samples.
- New run: 69.3% of busy time in 23 gap samples; top gap leaves are http `clearServerParserIncoming` (8.0 s), `writeCb` (3.6 s),
  drizzle `buildLimit` (12.7 s). The top self-time frames of the duration view are gap leaves, so they are not hot code either.
